Closing Circle Supper Club
Tonight How It Works

A dinner you have to find.

Closing Circle is one long table that moves. Each night it is set in a different Dallas neighbourhood, in a room that works by day and sits empty after dark: a print shop, a florist's back room, a potter's studio. Sixteen guests, five courses, one seating at 7:30 pm, $85 a seat.

Why the Address Is Hidden

A restaurant with a sign gets judged before you sit down. We would rather you arrive curious. So the address stays secret, and a circle on the map does the inviting: wide in the morning, tighter by lunch, a few streets across by late afternoon. By the time the door appears at 6:30 pm you have spent a day wondering about one part of the city, and you walk in already knowing the neighbourhood.

It also keeps the table small. Only people holding a seat have a reason to watch the circle close, and the hosts who lend us their rooms never have a crowd at the door.

Rooms That Sleep at Night

Most shops and studios in Dallas are lit for eight hours and dark for sixteen. The rent is paid either way. We set our table in those dark hours. A bookshop that closes at six can seat sixteen between its shelves by half past seven, and be a bookshop again by morning.

The host is paid for the evening and meets sixteen people who had never walked in before. Guests eat somewhere no restaurant could build. We bring the table, the kitchen and the cleaning crew, and leave the room as we found it.

Have a room that is empty after six? Ask the chat helper on this page about hosting a night.

10:30 pmThe table is cleared and tomorrow's circle appears.

House Rules

  • Seats are sold ahead, by the seat. There is no walk-in table.
  • Tell us about allergies and dietary needs when you reserve. With two days' notice the kitchen can cook vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free versions of every menu.
  • Please do not share the door before dinner. The hosts lend us their rooms on trust.
  • Arrive between 7:15 and 7:30 pm. The first course is served to the whole table at once.
  • Cancel up to 48 hours ahead for a full refund. After that your seat can be given to a friend.
