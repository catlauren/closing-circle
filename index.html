How to Find the Table

  1. Reserve a seat. Ask the chat helper in the corner of this page about dates, the menu and dietary needs.
  2. Watch the circle. It opens at 8:00 am, about 15 miles wide, and tightens all day. The kind of room is no secret; the street is.
  3. Knock at 7:30. At 6:30 pm the map shows the door. Dinner is five courses at one long table.
